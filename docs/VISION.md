# VISION.md

> The **why/what** of PoliPulse — who it's for, the one job it does, and what it is *not*.
> Product thinking lives here; durable operational facts live in `PROJECT-FACTS.md`.
> Drafted with the maintainer 2026-06-09. Edit freely — this is a thinking tool, not a contract.

## The one user
**An ordinary, politically-engaged voter** trying to figure out which candidates and parties
actually align with them — not journalists, not campaign staff (those may benefit, but we
optimize for the voter first).

## The one core job
**Alignment matching:** take a voter's actual positions and match them to candidates/parties
by *those candidates' real positions and records*. If PoliPulse did only this — and did it
credibly — it would still be worth using.

## The one thing it must do well
Produce an alignment result the voter **trusts**. Trust is the product. A slick quiz on top of
wrong data is worse than nothing here.

## Riskiest assumption (the thing that sinks it if wrong)
**That the underlying data is accurate.** Positions, votes, and finance data must reflect
reality. If the match is built on bad data, credibility collapses and the core job fails. This
is why "data accuracy" is the #1 roadmap priority and the ship gate — the vision and the
roadmap point at the same risk.

## Explicitly OUT of scope for v1
- **Social auto-posting (X/TikTok) and Remotion video cards.** Nice-to-have; parked so the
  core (accurate alignment matching) stays sharp. Revisit only after the data + match are
  trustworthy.

A voter can take the alignment quiz and get a result they find **credible and explainable**
