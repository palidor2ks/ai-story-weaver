> directional score, and which way each leans. Per
> [`poliscore-methodology.md`](./poliscore-methodology.md), every entry is published with source +
> rationale and is open to challenge. **Nothing here is final** — directions marked *[verify]* need
> confirmation against the bill text before they can score anyone.

> Built from the contested votes (NC delegation split Yea/Nay) sampled 2026-06-16. The sample is
> deliberately shown *with its noise* so the curation rules are visible in action.

  need a deeper pull (final-passage election/ethics/appropriations-process bills), or it shows as
